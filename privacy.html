Transparency

Privacy

TOV4R STUDIO is a static website. Its new catalog has no account registration, checkout, upload form, or newsletter form.

Data and local storage

Search and filters run locally in your browser. The site uses a service worker and browser cache to make some assets available offline. You can remove site data through your browser settings.

Third parties

Google Fonts may receive normal network requests when fonts load. GitHub and other external websites may collect technical visit information when you follow their links. The current studio homepage does not load an audience analytics script.

The archived gaming page

The optional legacy gaming page uses its own older media, embeds and optional analytics integrations. Those external services may load network resources when you open that page.

Downloads

Theme CSS and HTML templates are hosted as static files on this website. No purchase or personal details are required to download them.
